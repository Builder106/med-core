# MedCore: Cross-Border Go-to-Market Strategy (U.S. to Africa)

A practical roadmap for marketing, piloting, and distributing the MedCore platform across target African healthcare markets (e.g., Kenya, Nigeria, Ghana) while based remotely in the United States.

---

## 1. Leverage the Yale AIS Credential & Trust Anchors

Healthcare is relationship-driven and risk-averse. Institutional credibility opens doors that cold outreach cannot.

- **Award Collateral**: Feature the Yale Africa Innovation Symposium (YAIS IV) Technology & AI Track win, live demo link, and screenshots prominently on a 1-page executive brief and pitch deck.
- **YAIS & Academic Alumni Networks**: Reach out directly to symposium judges, mentors, and peers. Many are public health practitioners, founders, and physicians stationed in Nairobi, Lagos, and Accra.
- **Healthcare Diaspora Associations**: Network within diaspora physician groups such as the Kenyan Medical Association in Diaspora and the Association of Nigerian Physicians in the Americas (ANPA). Many U.S.-based African doctors own, advise, or invest in private clinics and diagnostic centers in their home countries.

---

## 2. WhatsApp-First Remote Outreach Funnel

Email open rates for busy clinic managers in East and West Africa are low; clinical operations run almost entirely on WhatsApp.

- **Local Virtual Presence**: Configure a dedicated WhatsApp Business account using a local virtual number (e.g., Kenyan `+254` or Nigerian `+234` via Twilio/VoIP providers) with official MedCore branding, catalogue links, and automated response routing.
- **Short Video Teasers**: Record 60- to 90-second product demonstrations addressing specific clinic bottlenecks:
  - Scanning a patient's Health ID QR badge on a standard smartphone.
  - Ambient voice consultation scribing and instant SOAP-note generation.
  - Automated drug-drug interaction alerts and PIN-authenticated prescription issuance.
- **Targeted Practice Outreach**: Identify Medical Directors and practice managers of small-to-midsize outpatient centers on LinkedIn and direct channels, focusing on administrative time savings and duplicate-lab prevention.

---

## 3. Ground Presence: Campus Ambassador & Field Rep Model

Direct, hands-on onboarding drives the fastest conversion without requiring costly full-time staff.

- **Medical & Nursing Student Ambassadors**: Partner with local university medical students (e.g., University of Nairobi, Kenyatta University, University of Lagos) as part-time clinical liaisons.
- **Structured Pilot Playbook**:
  - In-person clinic visits with an Android tablet or smartphone.
  - 5-minute interactive walkthrough for the head doctor or practice administrator.
  - An onboarding offer: a **60-day, zero-risk free pilot** for their next 50 patient encounters.
  - Performance-based stipends or commissions per active clinic successfully onboarded.

---

## 4. Target Low-Hanging B2B Segments

Avoid public hospitals early on due to extended procurement timelines, bureaucratic approvals, and complex tender requirements.

- **Tier 1: Private Outpatient Clinics (Nairobi, Lagos, Accra)**:
  - Practices with 1 to 4 physicians managing 30–60 patients daily using paper files or fragmented spreadsheets.
  - Single decision-maker (the clinic owner/head physician) with a sales cycle typically under two weeks.
- **Tier 2: Community Pharmacies & Independent Diagnostic Labs**:
  - Value digital prescriptions and structured referrals to drive legitimate patient volume and reduce fraud.
  - Offer a zero-cost intake dashboard to receive referrals directly from onboarded clinics, accelerating network effects.

---

## 5. Accelerators, Innovation Programs, and Grants

Structured programs provide local regulatory navigation, operational support, and non-dilutive capital:

- **Regional HealthTech Accelerators**:
  - *Villgro Africa* (Nairobi-based healthtech incubator/early-stage funder).
  - *Investing in Innovation (i3)* (Pan-African access and health supply chain program funded by the Gates Foundation, AUDA-NEPAD, and WHO).
  - *CcHUB* / *Techstars Africa* (Lagos, Nairobi).
- **Non-Dilutive Global Health Grants**: Apply for funding tracks targeted at digital health infrastructure, maternal care coordination, and healthcare workforce augmentation.
